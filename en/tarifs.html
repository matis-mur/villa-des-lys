Everything you need to know

Good to know before booking

Villa des Lys is rented exclusively. No other guests share the property or grounds with you: the house is entirely yours for the duration of your stay, set within enclosed grounds with no overlooking neighbours. The minimum stay is three nights outside high season, and seven nights in July and August, when the villa is reserved several months in advance.

The private concierge welcomes every arrival and remains at your disposal throughout your stay. Restaurant recommendations — from village bistros to Michelin-starred tables — secure reservations, bespoke excursions, grocery delivery before your arrival, a private chef from one hundred and twenty euros per person for an intimate dinner under the pergola: every service adapts to your pace and your wishes.

Ideally located in Puyricard, the villa offers a rare base from which to explore Provence. Aix-en-Provence and its Cours Mirabeau are ten minutes away by car, the Calanques of Cassis thirty-five minutes, the Luberon and its hilltop villages forty-five minutes, the Camargue an hour and ten. Marseille, its Old Port and museums are reached in thirty-five minutes by motorway.

Since 2021, the house has gathered enthusiastic feedback from its guests: 4.9 out of 5 stars across thirty verified Airbnb reviews, with particular praise for the quality of the amenities, immaculate cleanliness, the warm welcome from the owners, and the attentive concierge service. Booking directly through the website or by telephone guarantees the best rate and a personal connection with the house.
