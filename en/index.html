In the heart of Provence

Around the Villa

Perched on the heights of Puyricard, Villa des Lys opens the door to a Provence where each day reads like a poem. Ten minutes away, the Cours Mirabeau and the shaded squares of Aix-en-Provence invite you for a stroll. Beyond, Mont Sainte-Victoire reveals itself in a quarter of an hour, the hilltop villages of the Luberon await forty-five minutes away, and the Calanques of Cassis are reached in thirty-five minutes along the coastal corniche.

Provence is as much to be lived as admired. Cyclists set out along the peaceful vineyard roads, walkers find a legendary playground at Sainte-Victoire, and wine lovers visit the estates of Palette and Bandol. The Aix-en-Provence golf club, just five minutes from the villa, offers an eighteen-hole course traced through pines and holm oaks.

The house lends itself to every way of being together — families with its trampoline, pétanque court, Stella table-football and secured saltwater pool; groups of friends finding within its 269 m² of renovated space the room to share a week. The villa is rented on an exclusive-use basis, by the week, from 1 April to 31 October, with private concierge service included.

Your questions, our answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can Villa des Lys accommodate?

Villa des Lys sleeps up to 8 guests across 4 bedrooms and a self-contained studio.

How far is the villa from the centre of Aix-en-Provence?

Villa des Lys is set in Puyricard, around a 10-minute drive from the centre of Aix-en-Provence.

When is the villa available to rent?

The villa is rented by the week from 1 April to 31 October, on an exclusive-use basis, with private concierge service included.

Is the villa suitable for family stays with children?

Yes — the house is designed with families in mind: trampoline, pétanque court, Stella table-football, secured saltwater pool, child-friendly bedrooms and a private concierge for shopping or a private chef on request.

How can I book the villa?

Book directly with the owner via the website form or by telephone. The villa is also available on Airbnb.
